# Medium policy and safe publishing

Goal: the author's account stays safe and his stories stay eligible for distribution. This skill is always applied together with `medium-writer`. If the MCP server is connected, also run `check_medium_policy` on every draft.

All rules below were read on the official pages on 2026-11-25 (pages updated Sept to Nov 2026). Policies change. If a rule matters to a decision, re-open the source and check its "Updated" date. Mark anything not found as UNVERIFIED.

## 1. AI rules (highest risk for this project)
- Medium is for human storytelling, not AI-generated writing.
- "AI-generated writing" means the majority of the content was created by an AI writing program with little or no edits, improvements, fact-checking or changes. Outlining, spelling, grammar and fact-checking help are not counted and need no disclosure.
- AI-generated writing (disclosed or not) cannot be paywalled. Accounts doing it may lose paywall status or Partner Program enrollment. Using exclusively AI-generated content to earn in the Partner Program is prohibited.
- Any story with AI-generated or AI-assisted text must say so, in a sentence within the first two paragraphs, for example: "This story was written with the assistance of an AI writing program." Without it the story gets Network Only distribution (followers and subscribers only).
- AI images are allowed with a caption saying they are AI-generated (for example "This image was created using an AI image creation program."). Original cover images or carefully chosen stock are better than AI art.
- Not allowed: AI rephrasing or summarizing existing content into a close derivative (plagiarism), easily disprovable hallucinated stories or statistics, AI content made only for SEO or affiliate ranking.
- Boost guidelines list "human-created, not appearing to be AI-generated" as a quality trait. General Distribution excludes "AI-generated content" as low-value.
- Medium says it uses detection tools plus human review. Never promise the author that a text will pass.

**Project rule:** agents produce research, outlines and rough drafts only. The author rewrites in his own words with his real details. Disclose AI assistance when any AI-written text remains. Publish free (not member-only) unless the text is substantially his own writing.

## 2. Distribution tiers
- **Network Distribution:** baseline for any story that breaks no rules. Shown to followers (and publication followers).
- **General Distribution:** matched to readers by interests. Disqualified by clickbait, unconstructive negativity, low-value content (AI-generated, sponsored or PR, signup or traffic gathering, derivative or generic summaries, link round-ups), NSFW, misinformation, tag spamming, mass mentions (example given: tagging 20 users), rules violations (copyrighted images without permission, unverified harmful claims).
- **Boost:** human curators, seven days a week. Traits: credible first-hand experience, real value, respect for the reader (not a sales pitch), non-derivative fresh perspective, craftsmanship (sourced, error-free, formatting, alt text, credits). Disqualifiers include misleading, sensational, generic or formulaic title, subtitle or cover, and NSFW.
- **English only:** stories in other languages are not Boosted or given General Distribution yet (as of the page). Urdu stories will stay Network Only.
- **Stories about Medium itself** (Partner Program, earnings, Boost, writing strategies on Medium) are Network Distribution by default and are not eligible for the paywall. Tag them `medium-meta`.
- Medium does not fact-check but does not give General Distribution to stories it recognizes as misinformation.

## 3. Rules that apply to every story
- **Spam or misuse** (removed without notice, often suspension): content mainly to drive traffic or SEO to an external site; scraping and reposting for gain; duplicate content; clipped stories linking elsewhere; mass interactions (follows, claps, highlights, notes), often automated; repeated promotion via replies or mentions; bounty or brigade campaigns; **using or re-using content templates with slight modifications across multiple posts**.
- **Duplicate content:** no duplicate copies of the same story, from one or several accounts, public or unlisted. Cross-posting your own blog is allowed if you own the rights. Do not publish the same story on a second Medium account.
- **Plagiarism and copyright:** own the rights or have permission. Crediting a source does not replace permission. Mosaic paraphrasing counts. Copyright violations lead to suspension with no appeal. Cite with numbered superscripts or links, quote briefly.
- **Images:** use only images the author owns, has licensed, or Unsplash through the editor. Add alt text and credits. AI images need captions. Do not use copyrighted images without permission.
- **Deceptive conduct:** no misleading titles, tags, links or metadata. Disclose free goods or services given in connection with a post. No impersonation.
- **Promotion:** first-party promotion (own site, newsletter, GitHub, portfolio) is allowed. Third-party ads and sponsorships are not. Affiliate links are allowed with an FTC-style disclosure sentence.
- **Embeds and data collection:** no forms or embeds that collect emails or personal data on Medium. Links out must make clear the reader leaves Medium.
- **Privacy:** no private communications, non-public phone numbers, addresses, emails or confidential data. Never include client names, internal data, secrets or the author's phone or personal email.
- **Other:** no harassment, hate, graphic content, illegal hacking facilitation (credential theft, compromising personal data), pseudoscience or harmful disinformation. Security content must stay defensive.

## 4. Partner Program and paywall (the author wants to get paid)
Verified on the official pages on 2026-11-25:
- Eligibility: active Medium membership (a paid subscription the author buys himself), at least 1 published story, complete profile, bank in an eligible country, 18 or older, agree to the Partner Program Terms and the paywall content policy. **Pakistan is on the supported list** (page updated 2026-11-11).
- Enroll yourself at medium.com/partner-program (Join now). There is no application review: if you meet the requirements you are enrolled at once.
- Payouts need a connected Stripe account and verified taxpayer information (collected through Tipalti). Stripe requires the legal name (a pen name is fine on Medium), two-factor authentication by SMS or authenticator app, and payouts go only to a bank account. Medium does not give tax advice: use a tax professional.
- If the balance reaches $10 USD and payout settings stay incomplete for 30 consecutive days, enrollment becomes inactive and stories leave the paywall. A Stripe account inactive or unable to receive payouts for 180 consecutive days can forfeit unpaid earnings.
- The paywall is chosen **per story**: the "Paywall your story" checkbox when publishing, or "Manage paywall setting" on a published story. A default can be set in Publishing settings. Earnings come from member activity (reading, listening, claps, highlights, replies). The exact earnings formula page was not read, so no income figure can be promised.
- Not paywall-eligible: stories primarily about Medium or the Partner Program, and fully AI-generated writing.
- Prohibited: multiple accounts engaging with your own stories, reciprocal engagement, clapping, following or replying solely for engagement points, exclusively AI-generated content to earn. Consequences: revocation or suspension without notice or appeal.
- Agents must never buy a membership, enter bank, Stripe, tax or identity details, or click enroll or publish. The author does these himself.

### Earning track (paid stories) versus free track
| | Paid track (paywalled) | Free track |
|---|---|---|
| Who writes the text | The author, in his own words and voice | Author rewrites; AI-assisted text allowed with disclosure |
| What agents may do | Topic research, outline, source list, fact-check, grammar and style feedback, title options, policy check | Same, plus a rough draft the author rewrites |
| AI disclosure | Not needed for outline, fact-check, spelling, grammar help. Needed if any AI-written text or images remain | Required sentence in the first two paragraphs if any AI-written text remains |
| Risk | Paywalling AI-generated text can remove paywall status or enrollment | Network Only distribution if undisclosed |
| Meta stories about Medium | Never paywall | Allowed, tag `medium-meta`, Network Only by default |

Default for this project while the author wants income: **write paid-track stories himself, with agents as researcher, fact-checker and editor.** Use the free track for experiments and for stories where he wants wider reach with an AI-assistance disclosure.

## 5. Publishing pace
- No per-day story limit is documented in the pages above. A hidden cap on comments and editor actions has been reported by writers (anecdotal, UNVERIFIED), and one editor reported a block after 15 stories in a day.
- Spam rules punish templated, repeated and mass content. Our safe default: at most one story per day, aim for one or two per week, each clearly different in idea, structure and wording. Never publish many near-identical stories from the briefs.
- Never automate publishing, claps, follows, highlights or comments.

## 6. Pre-publish gate (run for every story, report pass or fail)
1. Idea is new: not a duplicate of any published story or draft; not a templated variant (compare with `author/stories-index.md` and other drafts).
2. Text is the author's: rewritten in his own voice with real details. No invented anecdotes or statistics. Every number has a primary source link and a date.
3. AI disclosure sentence is within the first two paragraphs if any AI-written text remains.
4. Paywall decision: paywall only if the text is substantially his own writing; never paywall meta-Medium or AI-generated text. Partner Program enrollment and payouts are done by the author.
5. Title and subtitle are specific and honest, not clickbait or formulaic. Cover image is original, licensed, or captioned AI image. Alt text added.
6. Language is English if he wants General Distribution or Boost.
7. At most 5 tags, all relevant. Mentions kept to a few and only where welcome.
8. Links: first-party promotion only, no third-party ads, affiliate disclosed, no forms collecting data.
9. No confidential client info, secrets, phone, personal email, private messages.
10. Quotes are short and credited. No close paraphrase of another article.
11. Pace: no more than one story that day, and no near-duplicate of a recent story.
12. Author reads it fully and clicks publish himself. Agents never publish.

## 7. How to answer policy questions
- Quote the rule briefly, name the page and its updated date, and say when you checked.
- If the pages do not answer it, say so. Do not guess. Suggest Medium support (help.medium.com) for confirmation.
- Never give legal or tax advice. Payout and tax questions go to Medium and an accountant.
