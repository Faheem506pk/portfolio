# Voice and style guide: Muhammad Faheem Iqbal

## Voice
- First person, plain, honest, humble. Learner who ships, not a guru.
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers, names and places. Small-step storytelling.
- Balanced takes: "X is useful, but..." Never absolute hype.
- Pakistan-based developer speaking to global readers. Simple English. Explain local context (PKR, university) briefly.
- Can be warm and a little informal ("Don't worry, bro" style quotes are fine when real).

## Keep
- Real figures and named people he has permission to name.
- Real code from his own repos.
- A clear takeaway the reader can act on.

## Fix (known habits from his 7 stories)
- Too many one-line paragraphs. Group 2 to 4 sentences into one paragraph.
- Recap lists at the end that repeat the story. Use 3 lines max.
- Titles that promise more than the body covers.
- Retelling the first-client and mentor story. It lives in "I Said Yes to a Web Development Project I Couldn't Build". Link to it.
- Typos: "EUR 100 EUR 120" style missing dashes.

## Hard rules
- Never write about the author's employer or client projects, or name them (see `neverMention` in `author/facts.json`). Not in stories, posts, scripts, bios or examples. Personal projects only.
- Never use an em dash. Use commas, periods, colons.
- Never invent anecdotes, metrics, benchmarks, tests, quotes, clients or credentials.
- Never reveal confidential client work, phone, personal email, or family details beyond what he already published.
- Disclose AI help when it was used (his thumbnails are AI-generated and he says so).
- Avoid AI tells: "delve", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "unlock", triple-abstract lists, "it's not X, it's Y" contrast tics. Vale `AiTells` rules flag these.

## Words he likes to use
real projects, small steps, learn by doing, mentor, ship, production, discipline, foundation, key vs door.
