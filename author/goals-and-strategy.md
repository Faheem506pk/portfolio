# Goals, audience and content strategy

## Positioning
Full stack software engineer who writes about AI-assisted JavaScript and TypeScript engineering from real repos, with a career-growth and IoT story from Pakistan as proof.

## Audience
1. Working developers (React, Next.js, TypeScript) adopting AI coding agents.
2. Students and junior developers in Pakistan and similar markets.
3. Hiring managers and freelance clients who find him through search.

## Goals (confirm with the author, ask if unknown)
- Build a recognizable personal brand across Medium, LinkedIn, X, YouTube.
- Grow followers and inbound opportunities (jobs, freelance).
- Establish authority on AI-native repositories and SKILL.md workflows.

## Content pillars
1. **AI-native engineering**: AGENTS.md, SKILL.md, MCP, quality gates, reviewing AI code. (Strongest lane.)
2. **JavaScript / TypeScript / React / Next.js in production**: lessons, gotchas, patterns, from non-confidential work.
3. **Career growth for juniors**: portfolio, AI and jobs, learning plans. Link to existing stories, avoid retelling them.
4. **IoT and builder projects**: ESP32, Alexa, PlantPulse, fingerprint lock.

## Distribution rule
One idea, many formats:
- Medium story (main, long form)
- LinkedIn post (hook plus 3 takeaways, link to story)
- X thread (5 to 8 posts)
- YouTube video script (screen-recorded demo of the same idea)
- Short clips if applicable

## Weekly rhythm (suggested)
1 Medium story, 1 LinkedIn post per story plus 1 standalone, 1 X thread, 1 YouTube video every 2 to 4 weeks.
